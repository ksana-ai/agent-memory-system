# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Protect private information and follow coordinated security disclosure.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope and evidence standards.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, and the
publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to a repository maintainer through the
owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens the
project or its participants. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

This project-specific code of conduct may be replaced by a formally adopted
community standard as the contributor community grows.
